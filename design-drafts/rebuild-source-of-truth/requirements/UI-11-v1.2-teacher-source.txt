Here is the complete, interactive, testable HTML5 prototype for UI-11: Curriculum & SOP Mapping Studio (/trainer/curriculum).

This updated studio equips workshop facilitators (Võ Từ Đức) and L&D Admins with a complete Lab Lifecycle & Governance Authoring Hub. It includes the Create New Lab Wizard, Inline Cohort Creation, Date & Time Scheduling Controls, 4-State Lifecycle Status Management (Draft → Published/Scheduled → Active → Inactive/Archived), and tabbed Basic vs. Advanced Metadata Forms (including customizable AI Judge rubric weights).
